## 1. Overview & Test Scope

The **Client & Patient Directory Module** (`/admin/patients`) is the core master patient index (MPI) of the Amerck Care Electronic Medical Record (EMR) platform. It provides clinical healthcare providers, physicians, and administrative staff with comprehensive tools to search, filter, register, review, and manage patient profiles and demographic records.

### Key Functional Capabilities:
1. **Master Directory Table:** Paginated clinical view of registered patients with core identifiers: HIN No., Patient Name, Age, NIC No., Mobile No., Emergency Contact, and Status.
2. **Real-Time Directory Search:** Live keyword querying by Patient Name, National Identity Card (NIC), and Mobile Number with instant backend query dispatch.
3. **Pagination & Volume Traversal:** High-volume pagination controls handling tens of thousands of client records across thousands of pages (`Previous`, `<current>/<total>`, `Next`).
4. **Patient Registration & Lookup Wizard:** Modal-driven identifier lookup validating NIC and Mobile numbers before launching new registration workflows.
5. **New Patient Registration (`/admin/patients/edit/new`):** Complete patient onboarding with pre-filled identifier parameters from search context (`nic=...`, `mobile=...`, `from=...`).
6. **Demographics & Profile Editing (`/admin/patients/edit/:id`):** Full profile management covering Personal Info, Contact Details, Guardian Info, and Physical Measurements.
7. **Physical Measurements & BMI Auto-Calculation:** Instant biometric calculation of Body Mass Index (BMI) from Height and Weight inputs.
8. **Medical History, Allergies & Clinical Notes:** Longitudinal clinical problem logging, allergies recording, active medications, and clinical comment threads.
9. **Consent Form Workflow:** Tracking and signing of digital clinical consent agreements.
10. **Bi-Directional Cross-Module Navigation:** Seamless routing between Clients Directory, Care View (`/admin/prescription`), Admissions (`/admin/admissions`), Telemedicine, and Settings.

> - Store credentials strictly in `.env` files protected by `.gitignore`.
